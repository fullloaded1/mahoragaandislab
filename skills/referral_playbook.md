# Referral Playbook (B2B Lab Procurement)

Consultative referral framework adapted from referral marketing best practices for Indonesian institutional lab procurement.

---

## 1. The Core Principle: Earned Referrals Only

In institutional B2B sales, referrals are not generated by aggressive viral tactics or cash bounties. They are earned strictly through flawless delivery, dependable equipment performance, and responsive after-sales support.

- **Never ask prematurely**: Never ask for a referral during initial outreach, negotiation, or before the client has experienced value.
- **The Trigger Moment (Post-BAST)**: Only ask after delivery, installation, commissioning, user training, and signing of the official Berita Acara Serah Terima (BAST).
- **The Relationship Threshold**: The client must express explicit satisfaction with equipment operation or customer service before any referral request is made.

---

## 2. Anti-Gratification & Anti-Bribery Compliance (Strict Guardrails)

In Indonesia, public institutions (instansi pemerintah, balai penelitian, BRIN), state-owned enterprises (BUMN), and public universities (PTN/BLU/BHMN) operate under strict anti-corruption laws (UU Tindak Pidana Korupsi and KPK Anti-Gratifikasi rules).

- **Strictly Prohibited**:
  - NO cash rewards, referral commissions, kickbacks, gift cards, or personal incentives to officials, procurement committees (PPK/Pokja), lab heads, or lecturers.
  - Never offer anything that could be construed as personal gratification or conflict of interest.
- **Permissible Institutional Value**:
  - Complimentary preventive maintenance check for the institution's existing equipment.
  - Priority scheduling for certified technician visits or calibration service.
  - Complimentary access to technical training materials or application notes for lab staff.

---

## 3. How to Ask: Low-Pressure, Consultative Framework

Frame the request as helping peers solve common laboratory challenges (such as long indent times or missing documentation):

### Timing
- **Day 7–14 after BAST**: Send a courtesy check-in confirming equipment is running smoothly.
- Once the customer confirms satisfaction, introduce the polite referral ask.

### Script: University / Research Lab (Post-BAST Satisfaction)
> *"Alhamdulillah, senang mendengar unit oven dan timbangan analitiknya sudah beroperasi lancar untuk praktikum mahasiswa, Pak Budi.*
>
> *Izin bertanya secara santun, apakah ada rekan dosen atau kepala lab di fakultas lain (misalnya Farmasi atau Teknik Kimia) yang saat ini juga sedang merencanakan pengadaan atau butuh referensi spesifikasi teknis? Jika ada, kami sangat senang jika bisa dibantu kenalkan agar kami dapat membantu menyiapkan draf teknisnya. Jika belum ada, sama sekali tidak masalah, Pak. Terima kasih banyak atas kepercayaan Bapak. 🙏"*

### Script: Industrial / QC Lab (Post-Installation)
> *"Terima kasih banyak atas feedback positifnya, Pak Budi. Kami lega unit autoclave-nya sudah lolos uji validasi QC.*
>
> *Boleh tahu Pak, apakah dari grup industri atau plant rekanan PT Contoh Lab ada divisi lab lain yang sedang membutuhkan ketersediaan unit ready-stock? Kalo berkenan, boleh kami dibantu kontak PIC-nya agar bisa kami kirimkan katalog teknis sebagai bahan pembanding. Terima kasih banyak sebelumnya. 🙏"*

---

## 4. Handling the Referral

1. **Obtain Explicit Permission**: Always confirm whether you may mention the referrer's name: *"Boleh kami sebutkan bahwa kami menyapa atas saran dari Pak Budi?"*
2. **First Touch to the Referred Contact**:
   - Keep it short (under 75 words), humble, and consultative.
   - Mention the peer context immediately:
     > *"Halo Pak Hendra, salam kenal. Saya Ahnaf dari tim penyedia lab. Pak Budi (Kepala Lab PT Contoh Lab) menyarankan kami menyapa Bapak, menginfokan unit ready-stock kami barangkali lab Bapak sedang membutuhkan perbandingan spesifikasi alat pengujian. Kalo berkenan, boleh saya kirimkan rangkuman spesifikasinya? 🙏"*
3. **If Declined or Ignored**:
   - Respect immediately. Never pressure either party.
   - Send sincere thanks to the referrer regardless of whether the lead converts.
